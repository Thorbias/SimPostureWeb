SimPosture

Usage Notes

As of: 20 July 2026

1. What SimPosture is

From a side-on photo, SimPosture estimates body-posture angles and compares them against a stored target profile. On that basis, the app gives general guidance on seating and posture.

2. Not medical advice

The app does not make a diagnosis, does not carry out an examination and is not a substitute for advice from a doctor, physiotherapist or other health professional. Its assessments and recommendations are general ergonomics guidance, not a remedy and not a therapy.

3. Use at your own risk

You decide whether and how to act on the guidance. Only change your posture as far as feels comfortable to you, not against pain.

If you have or develop health problems — such as back, neck or joint pain — do not rely on the app; seek advice from a doctor or physiotherapist. This applies in particular if you have a pre-existing condition.

4. Governing version

These usage notes are available in German and English. The German version is legally binding; in case of doubt, the German version prevails.

5. Changes

We may adjust these notes when the app or the legal framework changes. The version published on this page at the time of use applies.
