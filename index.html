What is SimPosture?

An ergonomics tool for sim racers. The app detects your body posture in the image, measures the angles that matter — backrest, knees, arms, distance to the wheel — and compares them against what's ideal for your vehicle class. A GT3 seating position is a different thing from a formula car; SimPosture knows the difference.

Who is it for?

For sim racers with their own rig and racing seat — from an entry-level build to a dedicated rig with a formula seating position. No ergonomics background needed; the app guides you.

How it works

  1. Capture — camera to the side of your rig, one photo in your typical seating position.
  2. Analyse — the app detects your posture and measures the relevant angles automatically.
  3. Improve — you get a rating with concrete tips, adjust your position and shoot again.

For every vehicle class

Road, GT3/GT, Prototype/LMP, Formula and Rally each have their own target values — from upright and close to the wheel to strongly reclined with raised legs. SimPosture rates against the class you actually drive.

Your data stays with you

Photo and analysis run entirely on your device. No upload, no servers, no sign-up, no tracking services — the app only needs the camera, and everything works offline after loading.
